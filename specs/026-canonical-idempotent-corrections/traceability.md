## Coverage gaps and evidence limits

- Every specified FR has a planned component, test, acceptance condition, and lot; this is planning coverage, not proof of feasibility or implementation. Activation remains blocked until the end-of-plan release gate; User Story 1 is not an independently releasable increment.
- The exact existing Ledger action allowlist and maximum reason/ID lengths must be confirmed against public type/validation helpers during implementation, then captured in exhaustive tests. No business semantics may be invented to fill gaps.
- The project has no mechanism to prove operating-system SavedVariables flush. FR-030/037 are satisfied by honest state reporting and later reload observation, not by a durable-write guarantee.
- Fengari does not certify Retail client networking, roster identity, SavedVariables flush behavior, or actual multi-client release safety. FR-041 requires separate in-game evidence.
