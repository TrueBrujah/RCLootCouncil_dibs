# Research: Development Catch-up

- **Decision**: Use a retrospective cross-feature corrective record.
  **Rationale**: 022/023 already contain plans/tasks; duplicating them would
  obscure ownership. **Alternative rejected**: mark unrelated work complete
  in their original tasks without explicit traceability.
- **Decision**: Preserve exact item links; never infer acquired bonuses from ID.
  **Rationale**: A base ring tooltip can show level 19 instead of the real reward.
  **Alternative rejected**: synthesize item level from difficulty or Myth track.
- **Decision**: Keep owned links transient and label them as previews.
  **Rationale**: Ownership does not prove Vault origin; two variants are ambiguous.
- **Decision**: Separate automated validation from Retail certification.
  **Rationale**: Synthetic claim APIs and transport doubles cannot prove actual
  client payloads or protected-frame behavior.
- **Decision**: Preserve existing test commands and dependency manifests.
  **Rationale**: No additional tooling is needed.
