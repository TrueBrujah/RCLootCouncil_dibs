RCLootCouncil_dibs 0.8.12

Working candidate updated 2026-11-15; not a publication or completed Retail certification.

Highlights
- Adds seasonal raid roster drafts, player registration, Officer review, and a read-only public roster.
- Adds versioned season rules and raid status definitions, including Raider and Core Raider signup paths.
- Adds explicit Main/Alt review, allocation preview and confirmed activation for approved season participants.
- Adds initial seasonal allocations, active-season allocation reconciliation preserving spent Dibs, and completion/archive with retained history.
- Adds an in-game calendar control for season dates and registration scheduling.
- Replaces the old guild-wide allocation path with season-scoped workflows for approved participants.
- Keeps the Player and Officer interfaces responsive and fixes the Retail Officer UI upvalue warning.
- Repairs Great Vault capture for supplemental reward bundles, localized self-loot without a sender GUID and delayed item data.
- Shows Great Vault capture state and its latest runtime result in PlayerUI; confirmed acquisitions synchronize to authorized Officer Vault Review views.
- Preserves deleted-ledger tombstones through restore/import and prevents presentation profiles from changing guild authority.

Compatibility and current limits
- Existing ledger and historical records are preserved. The seasonal roster uses an additive SavedVariables subtree.
- Seasonal roster synchronization uses Guild chat and requires clients to support the seasonal roster capability.
- Activation is available through the reviewed preview, reason and explicit confirmation by the authorized accounting coordinator. A preview alone allocates nothing.
- Activation manifests carry compact dependencies within the existing 8,192-byte quota; incomplete dependencies must not activate a season.
- Guild-wide allocation reconciliation is hidden; do not use rank changes to reconcile the entire guild roster.
- Great Vault capture requires Dibs active at claim time and the Pre-Dibs module enabled. Offered choices and old unobserved claims are not recorded automatically; Vault acquisitions do not spend Dibs.
- Real Retail single/two-client validation remains pending. Canonical dispute correction writes remain disabled with outstanding implementation/recovery tasks; this candidate does not certify that feature.
- Test procedure: docs/audits/0.8.12-TESTS-RETAIL-FR.md (repository).
