# Player FAQ

**Why can’t I create a Pre-Dib?** The item may be unsupported, the season may be inactive, the request mode may disallow public requests, or your character may not pass the configured eligibility policy. Ask an officer for the displayed reason.

**Why did my request not win?** A request is an eligibility/accounting signal. RCLootCouncil voting and the authorized award process still decide the item winner.

**Why did my balance not change after a boss?** Boss kills and requests do not create automatic grants or uses. Officers record seasonal allocations and qualifying awards explicitly.

**Why did a Vault item not consume a Dib?** Vault acquisitions are recorded for visibility and eligibility; they are not ledger consumption.

**Can I move an item between raids?** No. Synchronization can reconcile permitted accounting/request metadata, but the item and live RC vote stay in the raid where they occurred.

**What should I do if history shows a wrong date or response?** Save the row details and ask an officer to review the original RC evidence. Officers can annotate and confirm or reject an ambiguous historical row.

**What does an RC unavailable/degraded message mean?** The optional RCLootCouncil integration is missing or could not verify a capability. Local Dibs balance/history remains usable; live RC-dependent actions may be unavailable until the addon/API is ready.
